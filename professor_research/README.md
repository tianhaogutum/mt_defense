# TUM / LMU Symbolic AI, AI and LLM Professors

Last updated: 2026-09-15

This folder is a resumable research notebook for identifying current professors at
TUM and LMU whose research substantially involves symbolic AI, artificial
intelligence / machine learning, NLP, foundation models, or LLMs.

## Inclusion rules

- Include current professors whose official university profile, chair, lab, or
  research-center page explicitly establishes a substantial connection.
- Include joint, adjunct, honorary, and emeritus professors only in clearly
  labelled sections; do not mix them with current core faculty.
- Put ambiguous cases in `review_queue.md` until an official source resolves them.
- Prefer official TUM/LMU pages. Research-center pages are useful for discovery,
  but the university affiliation and title must be independently checked.

## Files

- `progress.md`: checkpoint and next actions.
- `tum.md`: verified TUM professors and evidence.
- `lmu.md`: verified LMU professors and evidence.
- `review_queue.md`: candidates and exclusions awaiting resolution.
- `sources.md`: official directories and systematic search coverage.

## Status labels

- `verified`: official title, current affiliation, and relevant research verified.
- `partial`: official affiliation verified; relevance or current title needs more evidence.
- `review`: candidate discovered but not yet verified.
- `excluded`: checked and outside the stated scope, with reason recorded.
